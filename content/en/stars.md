**Stars** is a mobile application designed to help parents motivate their children through a star-based reward system. With **Stars**, parents can manage and track their children’s activities while encouraging goal-setting and positive behavior.  

#### Key Features

- **Customizable star management:** Parents can grant stars as rewards for achievements or good behavior and keep a clear record of each child’s star balance.  
- **Detailed activity logs:** The app maintains a history of how stars were awarded or removed, ensuring transparency in the process.  
- **Flexible reward system:** Children can exchange their accumulated stars for rewards agreed upon with their parents, such as gifts or privileges.  
- **User-friendly and secure interface:** Designed to be easy to use, with secure data storage both locally and in the cloud.  
- **Privacy assured:** No data is shared with third parties, keeping your family’s information protected.  

**Stars** is the perfect tool for parents and children to work together toward shared goals, strengthening communication and mutual appreciation.
