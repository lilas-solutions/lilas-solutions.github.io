**Lilas Solutions SpA** ("we," "us," or "our") provides this account deletion guide to help users permanently remove their **Mini Business** account and associated data in compliance with Google Play Data safety policies.

---

## How to Delete Your Mini Business Account

To delete your Mini Business account, you must contact us directly via email. Account deletion is not available through the app interface.

**Email Request Method:**  
Send an email to **contact@lilas.solutions** with the subject line "Account Deletion Request - Mini Business" and include:
- Your registered email address
- Confirmation that you want to permanently delete your account

---

## What Data Gets Deleted

When you delete your Mini Business account, the following information is **permanently removed**:

### Personal Information
- Email address (used for account authentication)

### Business Data
- All sales records and transaction details
- Purchase records and expense tracking data
- Client information (names, contact details, addresses)
- Provider information (names, contact details, addresses)
- All reports and summaries generated by the app
- Custom business settings and preferences

### Account Information
- Account authentication data
- App usage statistics and preferences

---

## Data Retention Policy

### Immediate Deletion (Within 30 days)
- All personal information (email address)
- All business data (sales, purchases, clients, providers)
- Account authentication data
- App settings and preferences

### Legal Compliance Retention
In certain circumstances, we may retain minimal data for legal compliance purposes:
- Basic transaction records (if applicable for paid features)
- Data required for legal investigations or disputes
- Information necessary to comply with applicable laws

**Maximum Retention Period:** 7 years for legal compliance purposes only

### What Cannot Be Recovered
Once your account is deleted:
- All business records and transaction data are permanently lost
- Client and provider information cannot be recovered
- Reports and summaries cannot be regenerated
- Account reactivation is not possible with the same data
- You will need to create a new account to use Mini Business again

---

## Processing Time

**Account deletion requests are processed within 30 days** of confirmation. You will receive an email confirmation once the deletion is complete.

**Important Note:** During the processing period, your account will be deactivated and inaccessible, but final data removal may take up to 30 days to complete across all our systems.

---

## Data Downloaded Before Deletion

If you wish to download your business data before deletion, please contact us at **contact@lilas.solutions** with the subject line "Data Export Request - Mini Business" at least 7 days before requesting account deletion.
