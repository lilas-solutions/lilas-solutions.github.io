Support your studies with **Study-bot**, generating quizzes with multiple choice, open questions, timed sessions, and getting final scores, feedback on your answers, and the areas you need to reinforce.

#### What is Study-bot?

**Study-bot** is a mobile application developed to transform the way you study, making personalized learning and smart time management easier. With the power of artificial intelligence, you can generate different quizzes based on the subject you need to study, separated into subtopics for as much detail as possible and as close to a real test as it gets. The quizzes can be multiple choice, open questions, or timed. This allows you to get a final score and see which areas are your weakest, so you can keep improving your study.

#### Who is it for?

**Study-bot** is designed for students of all ages who are looking for a modern tool to prepare for their exams more efficiently. It's ideal for those who need daily help as well as those who want to boost their independent study.

#### How does it work?

- **Enter your class material:** presentations, digital information, summaries
- **Quiz generator:** test your knowledge with the quiz generator; more than 200 questions—multiple choice and open—that can help you check your learning progress
- **Different types of questions:** can be multiple choice and/or open, separated by subtopics so you cover everything you've learned in detail. Also, if you don't understand an answer, you can ask the smart chat for a deeper explanation
- **Adapted to your level:** questions are tailored to your age and education level, so the challenge is regulated and appropriate
- **Final score:** depending on the quiz mode you choose, you'll get your final score

#### Advantages of using Study-bot

- Interactive and personalized learning
- 24/7 assistance available
- Quizzes similar to real learning
- Feedback to reinforce study areas
- Real-time evaluation of your learning

#### Why choose Study-bot?

**Study-bot** combines artificial intelligence, pedagogy, and mobile technology in an easy-to-use app that's always by your side. If you want to study better, solve doubts in seconds, and get realistic exam questions, **Study-bot** is your ideal companion on the learning journey.
