**Lilas Solutions SpA** ("we," "us," or "our") provides this account deletion guide to help users permanently remove their **Study-bot** account and associated data in compliance with Google Play Data safety policies.

---

## How to Delete Your Study-bot Account

### For Adult Users (18 years and older)

To delete your Study-bot account, you must contact us directly via email. Account deletion is not available through the app interface.

**Email Request Method:**  
Send an email to **contact@lilas.solutions** with the subject line "Account Deletion Request - Study-bot" and include:
- Your full name
- Your registered email address
- Confirmation that you want to permanently delete your account

### For Minor Users (Under 18 years)

Since accounts for users under 18 require parental/guardian consent, account deletion must be requested by the parent or legal guardian who originally created the account.

**Email Request Method:**  
The parent/guardian must email **contact@lilas.solutions** with the subject line "Minor Account Deletion Request - Study-bot" including:
- Child's full name
- Parent/guardian's name
- Registered email address
- Confirmation that you are the authorized parent/guardian
- Request to permanently delete the child's account

## What Data Gets Deleted

When you delete your Study-bot account, the following information is **permanently removed**:

### Personal Information
- Nickname
- Email address (used for Google OAuth2 authentication)
- Gender (if provided by user - optional)
- Study grade level (e.g., first grade, fifth grade, etc.)
- Profile preferences (optional generic icon and color used as avatar)

### Study Data
- All quiz results and scores
- Study session history
- Flash cards and study materials
- Progress tracking data
- Custom study content

### Account Information
- Google OAuth2 authentication data
- Account preferences and settings
- App usage statistics

---

## Data Retention Policy

### Immediate Deletion (Within 30 days)
- All personal information
- Study content and progress data
- Account authentication data
- Device information

### Legal Compliance Retention
In certain circumstances, we may retain minimal data for legal compliance purposes:
- Basic transaction records (if applicable for future paid features)
- Data required for legal investigations or disputes
- Information necessary to comply with applicable laws

**Maximum Retention Period:** 7 years for legal compliance purposes only

### What Cannot Be Recovered
Once your account is deleted:
- All study progress and results are permanently lost
- Custom study materials cannot be recovered
- Account reactivation is not possible with the same data
- You will need to create a new account to use Study-bot again

---

## Processing Time

**Account deletion requests are processed within 30 days** of confirmation. You will receive an email confirmation once the deletion is complete.

**Important Note:** During the processing period, your account will be deactivated and inaccessible, but final data removal may take up to 30 days to complete across all our systems.

---

## Data Downloaded Before Deletion

If you wish to download your data before deletion, please contact us at **contact@lilas.solutions** with the subject line "Data Export Request - Study-bot" at least 7 days before requesting account deletion.
