Need help with **Mini Business**? This page covers common questions and quick fixes for plans, sync, AI document scanning, and team collaboration. If you still need assistance, contact us at **contact@lilas.solutions**.

---

### Plans & Features

- **Basic (free)**: 1 owned business, offline-first, no cloud sync, no AI scans, solo use. You can join Pro businesses as a member.
- **Pro (paid)**: Up to 3 owned businesses, cloud sync + multi-device, unlimited team members, 100 AI document scans per business per month.
- **Ownership vs membership**: A user can own up to the plan limit; membership in other businesses is unlimited. If an owner’s Pro subscription lapses, that business reverts to Basic features.

---

### Getting Started

1. Sign in with Google (and Apple on iOS).
2. Create your first business or join with an invitation code.
3. For Pro: ensure subscription is active, then wait for the first sync to finish.

---

### Sync & Offline

- **Offline mode**: Basic stays offline; Pro works offline and syncs when online.
- **Sync states**: Initializing → syncing → ready. If you see “Waiting for network…”, check connectivity and keep the app open.
- **Conflicts**: If a record changed elsewhere, choose to keep your version or accept the server version.
- **Business switching**: Changing business restarts data loading; allow a few seconds for sync to complete.

---

### AI Document Scanning

- Available on **Pro** only; 100 scans per business per month.
- Supported: photos (JPEG/PNG) and PDFs for sales or purchases.
- Workflow: Tools → Scan Document → select type → upload → review extracted items → save or discard.

---

### Team Collaboration

- **Invite members** (Pro only): Business → Members → Create invitation → share the code. Codes typically expire in 7 days.
- **Join a business**: Use the invitation code; email must match the invitation if specified.
- **Permissions**: Members can add/edit business data; owners manage subscriptions and member access.

### FAQ

#### Why can’t I sync my data online?
Basic plan is offline-only. Upgrade to Pro and let the initial sync finish on a stable connection.

#### What happens if my subscription expires?
Owned businesses downgrade to Basic: sync stops, AI scans disabled, and team invites blocked. Reactivate Pro to restore features.

#### How do I invite teammates?
Pro only. Go to Business → Members → Create invitation, share the code, and ask them to join from “Join business”.

#### Why do I see a sync conflict?
Another device edited the same record. Pick whether to keep your version or accept the server version.

#### How many AI scans do I have?
100 scans per month per business on Pro. The counter resets monthly. Basic has 0.

#### Can I use the app on multiple devices?
Yes on Pro. Basic is single-device offline because it has no cloud sync.

#### Why did my AI scan fail?
Common causes: poor image quality, unsupported format, or monthly quota exceeded. Try a clearer photo or check quota.

#### How do I switch businesses?
Tap the Switch Business button in the main menu and select another business. Data reloads and may take a few seconds to sync.

#### Can I leave a business I joined?
Yes. Leaving removes your access to that data. Owners cannot leave their own business; they can delete it instead.

#### How do I delete my account?
See the account deletion instructions here: [Delete your Mini Business account](minibiz-delete-account).
