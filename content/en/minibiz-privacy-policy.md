*Effective Date: December 19th, 2025*

At **Lilas Solutions**, your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information. By using Mini Business, you agree to the practices outlined in this policy. If you have any questions, please contact us at **contact@lilas.solutions**.

---

### 1. Information We Collect

**Personal Information**
- Email address (used for authentication and identification)

**Business Data**
- Sales and purchase details: products, prices, payments, and delivery dates.
- Client and provider information: names, contact details, and addresses.
- Business settings: categories, initial balances, and preferences.

**Team Collaboration Data**
- Member names/emails for invitations and access control.

**AI Document Scanning Data (Pro only)**
- Images or PDFs you upload for extraction (sales/purchases). Content is sent to trusted AI processors (e.g., OpenAI) to generate structured suggestions.

**Usage and Quota Data**
- AI scan usage counts per business to enforce monthly quotas (100 scans per business on Pro).
- Basic service telemetry to ensure sync and licensing operate correctly (no advertising profiles).

---

### 2. How We Use Your Information

- Operate the application (transactions, reports, cash flow, multi-business management).
- Provide sync and multi-device access for Pro users.
- Process AI document scans and return suggested records for your review.
- Enforce plan limits, AI quotas, and business ownership limits.
- Maintain security, prevent abuse, and comply with legal obligations.
- We do not use your data for advertising or marketing profiles.

---

### 3. Data Storage and Security

- **Local Storage**: Data is stored on your device for offline use.
- **Cloud Storage**: For online features, data is stored securely in a private database with access controls.
- **AI Processing**: Documents you submit for AI extraction are transmitted to trusted processors solely to return suggested data. We do not retain these documents beyond the processing operation; processors may retain limited logs per their policies.
- **Security**: We use technical and organizational measures to protect against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

---

### 4. Data Sharing

- We do not sell your data.
- We share data only with service providers necessary to operate the app (e.g., hosting, AI processing). These providers are bound by confidentiality and data protection obligations.
- Team members of a business can see shared business data according to app permissions.

---

### 5. Data Retention and Deletion

- Account and business data are retained while your account is active or as needed to provide the service.
- AI usage counts are retained to enforce quotas and for billing/abuse prevention.
- Documents submitted for AI extraction are not kept after processing, except for minimal operational logs.
- You may request deletion of your account; deleting your account removes associated business data unless retention is required by law.

---

### 6. Your Responsibilities

- Keep your login and devices secure.
- Input accurate data and respect others’ data when collaborating.
- Obtain appropriate consent from team members you invite and from individuals whose data you store.

### 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be communicated through the application. Continued use of the app after such changes signifies your acceptance of the updated policy.
