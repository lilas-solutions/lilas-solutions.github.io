Keep track of your karting sessions with **Karting Log**: lap times, tracks, championships and your progress over time, all in one place.

#### What is Karting Log?

**Karting Log** is an app for karting drivers, and for the parents and coaches who follow them, to record every training, leisure, qualifying and race session. Log your best and average laps, the track you drove on, and the championship the session belongs to, and see how your times improve over time.

#### How does it work?

- **Record your sessions:** add the date, track, session type, position and lap times
- **Read results from a photo:** take a picture of the timing screen or printed timing sheet, and artificial intelligence reads the drivers and lap times for you to review and save
- **Import from CSV:** bring in sessions you already recorded in a spreadsheet
- **Drivers, venues and championships:** organize your data and add pictures to recognize them at a glance
- **Works without an account:** start right away in guest mode, and sign in with Google or Apple when you want to keep your data backed up and synced across your devices

#### Who is it for?

**Karting Log** is for anyone who drives karts, from occasional leisure drivers to drivers competing in championships, and for parents and coaches who keep track of several drivers.
